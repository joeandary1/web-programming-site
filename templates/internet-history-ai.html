History of the Internet

Introduction

The Internet is a global network that connects computers and devices around the world. It developed over many years through advances in networking, communication protocols, and computer technology.

Key Milestones

1969 — ARPANET

ARPANET connected several research computers in the United States.

Why it mattered: It was one of the first major packet-switched networks and became an important foundation for the Internet.

Source: https://www.internetsociety.org/internet/history-internet/brief-history-internet/

1983 — TCP/IP

ARPANET officially switched to TCP/IP.

Why it mattered: TCP/IP allowed different networks to communicate using the same system.

Source: https://www.internetsociety.org/internet/history-internet/brief-history-internet/

1983 — DNS

The Domain Name System was introduced.

Why it mattered: DNS allowed people to use domain names instead of remembering numerical IP addresses.

Source: https://www.ietf.org/

1986 — NSFNET

NSFNET connected universities and research centers.

Why it mattered: It helped expand Internet access and became an important Internet backbone.

Source: https://www.nsf.gov/

1995 — Commercial Internet

NSFNET was shut down and commercial Internet providers became more important.

Why it mattered: This helped the Internet expand to businesses and the general public.

Source: https://www.nsf.gov/

Three Key Internet Technologies

Packet Switching

Splits data into packets that can travel across a network.

TCP/IP

Allows different computers and networks to communicate.

DNS

Converts domain names into IP addresses.
